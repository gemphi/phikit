# Phiogy - specs

Each piece: what it must do, what Apple calls the like of it, what was
read before it was written, and where it is tested. Tests are under
`src/test`, in the folder of the code. The Node line is
`puikit/app/phiora-server/src/call.mjs`, written `call.mjs` below with
its line; its tests are `puikit/app/phiora-server/test/call.test.mjs`.

## Not met

| Piece | Must | Why not yet |
|---|---|---|
| a camera | send what a person looks like | built and never run: the machine it was made on has no camera |
| every scene of a host | share several screens at once, or a window | one screen is shared at a time, as a page shares one |
| a relay | let two networks meet | no TURN server; `data/ice.tsv` is empty |
| a forwarding unit | hold a room above 8 | a call is a mesh |
| messages | reach the other person | the board holds calls only |
| who a caller is | be the person Phiora knows | every page is a guest by its cookie |
| a record | keep joins and leaves | which `philog` is the user's to say |
