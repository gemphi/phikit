## Done

Both sides are now read, with the node's Factor and blend mode, and blended per pixel by `ramp_blend` (`blenkernel/intern/material.cc`, all nineteen `MA_RAMP_*` modes) in the kernel. Measured on the BASKETBALL-COURT scene, whose floor is exactly this — planks from a banded Wave through a Color Ramp mixed 0.35 with the grain of a Noise over a Mapping scaled (1, 22, 1) — and whose floor rendered perfectly flat before (`tests/nodes_truth.rs`, the MixedWood plane of NODES.blend)
