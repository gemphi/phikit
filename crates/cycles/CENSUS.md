# Census of the test scenes

Generated by `cargo run -p cycles --release --example census`; do not edit.

763 scenes with a reference were read. 633 have a lamp (gate 3), 130 have none. Of those 130, 28 need nothing beyond gate 1.

Kinds are Blender node idnames, `closure ...` rows are the closures a node makes (the Principled BSDF's lobes by which inputs are linked or off their default), `WorldBackground` is the world's colour. A world's own node graph is not exposed by phiphx (see PLAN.md); its image or sky counts as `ShaderNodeTexEnvironment` or `ShaderNodeTexSky`. Node groups are not expanded here (`ShaderNodeGroup` is counted, its inner nodes are not).
