Brand guidelines · v1

One idea drawn three ways: a stem, a half-hexagon bowl, and a constant gap. The mark tilts it 30°, the letters keep it upright, and every piece of the system — the two colours, the spacing, the corner radius — is derived from that geometry rather than added to it.

01 · The mark

A P, mirrored, in two tones

canvas
24 × 24 units; the mark is drawn upright and rotated 30° clockwise about the centre
stem
2.4 wide, 4.5 → 19.5; a 1.1 core inset one gap inside it
bowl
half-hexagon C, 10.8 tall, band 1.3, slants at 30°, vertical side 2× a slant, corner radius 1.4
inner
solid half-hex one gap inside the C, cut flat one gap off the stem, corners 0.6
gap
0.4 everywhere: stem→C, stem→inner, C→inner, core→stem edge
colour
top bowl: orange C, blue inner · bottom bowl: blue C, orange inner · stem orange, core blue

The gap is the brand. Nothing touches: the bowls float beside the stem, the inner shape floats inside its C, and the core floats inside the stem. If two shapes meet, the mark is wrong.

02 · Colourways

Two layers, not four colours

In the app the mark is two SVG layers painted in brand_warm and brand_cool, so a theme can retint it without redrawing. The split follows the colourway (warm = top C + bottom inner + stem; cool = top inner + bottom C + core), not the geometry.

03 · Palette

Blue and orange, on ink or paper

Phiber Blue#5B9CF0cool layer: top inner, bottom C, stem core
Phiber Orange#F28C28warm layer: top C, bottom inner, stem
Ink#161B22dark ground, app chrome
Paper#F3F5F8light ground, wordmark on ink
Slate#2A3340panels, dividers
Mist#C9D1DCsecondary text on ink

Orange leads: it is the stem and the outer C on top. Blue answers it. Never swap them on one side only — the swapped variant was explored and retired; the official mark is this one.

04 · Clear space & size

Room to float

clear space
a quarter of the mark's width on every side; nothing enters it
minimum
20 px in UI (the header anchor), 16 px favicon in mono, 24 mm in print
rounded square
corner radius 22.5% of the tile (5.4 of 24), mark centred, no padding change
rotation
30° clockwise, always; the upright form is the letter P, not the mark
05 · Letterforms

PHIBER, drawn with the same six rules

One stroke weight (2.0), stems from 4.5 to 19.5, every detached piece one gap (0.4) from its neighbour, every bowl a half-hexagon with the mark's rounded corners. The letters stay upright — the tilt belongs to the mark.

tracking
2.6 units between letter extents; the lockup sets the wordmark 26 units right of the mark's canvas
lockup
mark first, wordmark cap-height = mark canvas height; use on ink or paper only
files
wordmark.svg, font/P.svg … R.svg, lockup-dark.svg, lockup-light.svg
